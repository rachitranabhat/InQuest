## 3. What was wrong

Two kinds of problem: confirmed factual errors (the records contradict the claim) and unsupported interpretations (the claim goes beyond what the records show).

| # | Finding | Type | What the records show |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | F-13: "24.3% of the network's transactions" | **Confirmed factual error** | 24.3% = 9 of the subject's 37 transactions (EV-001262). |
| 2 | F-14: no history before the window | **Confirmed factual error** | A 49-purchase, 23-day baseline exists and is sufficient (EV-001227). |
| 3 | F-13: the jackpot week explains the pattern | **Unsupported interpretation** | The event covers 9 of 37 transactions, and those 9 are small. The 12 × $250 burst is on 10-12, after the event ended. Subject growth was 17.98× against 2.01× for the network. No record links the burst to the event. |
| 4 | F-15 and the Lead: familiar retailers make the activity ordinary | **Unsupported interpretation** | The retailer fact is true: 11 of 12 claims were at known retailers. It does not explain 12 identical $250 claims across 6 retailers in 18 minutes. |
| 5 | Alternative summary: "no definitive evidence of unusual behavior" | **Unsupported interpretation** | Fired indicators include frequency (EV-001220, 25/25) and repeated pattern (EV-001225, 10/10). |
| 6 | Lead `concern_reduced` | **Unsupported assessment** | It rests on #1, #3 and #4. The concern_reduced wording in our Lead prompt also didn't require a check that the explanation covers the peak. |

**Not caught by any check:**
- F-05 calls the 12 $250 records "repeated purchases"; the records label them Prize Claim.
- F-06 says "1 new retailer"; the records show 2 (EV-001245, EV-001247).

These are minor, but the current checks do not detect them.

## 5. What the tests prove and what remains untested

**Proven by tests:**

| Proven (deterministic) | Test (`backend/tests/test_agents.py`) |
|---|---|
| The network-share misreading and the missing-history contradiction from the real run are rejected; the 11 other findings are kept | `test_cross_check_rejects_network_share_misreading_and_history_contradiction` |
| Asking for history *beyond* the baseline is allowed | `test_missing_history_beyond_the_baseline_is_allowed` |
| A jackpot explanation is marked possible, not verified; the peak on 10-12 is noted as outside the event; the weight drops to weak; concern_reduced is flagged but not changed | `test_unsupported_ordinary_explanation_is_marked_possible_and_peak_outside_event_noted` |
| An alternative finding that denies anomalies is rejected | `test_alternative_finding_denying_anomalies_is_rejected` |
| The checks do not flag a consistent assessment | `test_cross_check_leaves_assessment_to_the_lead` |

The fixture `backend/tests/fixtures/agents_case18016.json` holds the original run's verified findings and the 8 evidence records they need.

**Results (2026-10-16, SQLite, no API key):**
- Agent tests: 20 passed (repeated 3×).
- Full backend suite: 114 passed, 3 skipped, 1 failed. The one failure was `test_api_start_runs_in_background_persists_and_keeps_history`, which is timing-flaky: two runs started in the same second can sort in either order. It passed on the 3 reruns. It is unrelated to these checks.

**Not tested or not shown:**
- **No live LLM run since the fix.** We do not know whether gpt-4o-mini would now assess differently.
- **No accuracy claim.** No labelled evaluation of investigation outcomes was run, so this report claims nothing about better fraud detection.
- **One case.** The checks were built from and replayed on one synthetic case.
- **Pattern-based checks.** They match wording, so a misreading phrased differently may get through.
- **Known gaps:** "familiar retailer" reasoning, transaction-type mislabels (F-05) and retailer count errors (F-06) are not checked.

## 6. Reproduce

From `fraud-investigation-assistant-v2` with the V2 venv active:

```powershell
# 1. Replay the stored live run through the current checks (read-only, no model calls)
python scripts\validate_investigation.py INV-2f790604b4

# 2. Regression tests (no network, no API key)
cd backend
$env:PRISM_DISABLE_DOTENV="1"; $env:OPENAI_API_KEY=""
python -m pytest -q tests/test_agents.py
```

The replay also accepts `--json <file>`, where the file is `{"result": …, "items": […]}`, for an exported copy of the run.
